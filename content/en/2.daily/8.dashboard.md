The **dashboard** is your landing page after sign-in. It answers one question: is
everything working?

Access: left menu → **Dashboard**

## The indicators

| Indicator | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Connected instances** | At least one, otherwise nothing sends |
| **Banned instances** | Must stay at zero |
| **Validated contacts** | Should grow as you import |
| **Active templates** | At least one active, otherwise no campaign can launch |
| **Groups** | Your commercial segments |

**7-day sending trend** — the volume sent each day. A sudden drop means a broken
instance or a stopped campaign.

## The getting-started checklist

While your account is new, a **Getting started** checklist lists the next step:
connect a number, pick a template, send a test, launch a first campaign. Follow it
in order, it tracks your progress.

## Quick actions

Shortcuts to the frequent actions: new campaign, new contact, new template.

## Statistics

The **Statistics** page (left menu → **Statistics**) goes further:

- **incoming messages** and **first replies**: how many customers answered you;
- **response rate** per campaign;
- **daily trends**;
- **top campaigns**, compared with the previous one and with similar campaigns;
- performance by **instance**, by **template** and by **group**;
- breakdown of incoming message types.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-chart-line"}
Analytics need a few days of activity to mean something. On a new account, the
figures are near zero: that is normal.
::

## What the dashboard is not

It does not replace the campaign page: to follow a send in detail, open
[Campaigns](/en/daily/campaigns). And it does not show billing: that is in
[Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans) and
[Subscription](/en/billing/subscription).

The **chatbot** replies in your place when a customer writes. Three engines are
available, from the simplest to the most advanced.

Access: left menu → **Chatbot**

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-lock"}
The chatbot is not included in the free Starter plan. It appears in the menu from
the Growth plan — see [Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans).
::

## The three engines

| Mode | How it works | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Rules** (recommended) | A keyword triggers a reply | Getting started, 90 % of needs |
| **Workflow** | A visual scenario: questions, branches, steps | Multi-step journeys (order tracking) |
| **Webhook** | KwikTalk calls your own service, which decides | Teams that already have a bot |

Start with **Rules**. Switch to Workflow when a rule is not enough.

## Rules

1. **Create a rule**: a name, one or several **keywords**, and the reply.
2. Choose the matching: an exact word or a word contained in the message.
3. **Activate** the rule.

Example: keyword `price` → "Our rates start at 19 000 FCFA per month. Which
formula interests you?"

Two rules matching the same message: the first activated wins. Write specific
keywords, and use a **fallback message** for everything else.

## Fallback and AI

**Fallback message** — sent when no rule matches. Never leave it empty: a message
without a reply is a customer lost.

**AI reply as a last resort** — if you enable it, messages none of your rules catch
are handed to an AI that answers from the instructions you wrote. Provide:

- an **instruction** describing your business, tone and what it must not say;
- nothing else: if you have no instruction, disable the AI.

The AI answers text only. It keeps the conversation's memory per customer.

## Guardrails

Automation that answers everything, all the time, gets your number banned. Four
settings control it:

| Setting | Role |
|---|---|
| **Cooldown** (minutes) | No two automatic replies to the same contact within this delay |
| **Maximum replies per day** | Global cap on automatic replies |
| **Time slots** | Hours during which the bot is allowed to answer |
| **Reply scope** | All, saved contacts only, or excluding group members |
| **Excluded numbers** | Numbers the bot must never answer |

Put a **cooldown of several minutes** and a **maximum of a few hundred replies per
day**: that is what separates a helpful bot from a spam machine.

## Test before activating

The chatbot offers a **test** area: type a message, see the reply, without writing
to WhatsApp. On a workflow, a **simulator** walks the scenario node by node.

Check at least: a message that matches a rule, a message that matches nothing, a
message sent at night (outside the time slots).

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Answers according to its settings |
| **Paused** | Temporarily stopped, settings kept |
| **Inactive** | Turned off |

Pausing is the right move when you take over a conversation by hand.

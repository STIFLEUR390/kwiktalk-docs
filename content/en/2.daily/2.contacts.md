Once your contacts are imported (see
[Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts)), this page is about keeping
them: fix a number, validate again, file into a group, delete.

Access: left menu → **Contacts**, and **Contact groups**

## The contact list

Each row shows the name, the number, the validation status, the groups and the
attached instance. Search filters on name and number; the group filter lets you
work on a subset.

### Actions on a contact

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| Open the record | Click the row |
| Edit name, number, groups | **Edit** |
| Validate a number | **Validate** on the row, or **Validate selection** after ticking several rows |
| Delete | **Delete** on the row |

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
**Changing a contact's number resets its status to "Pending".** The previous
validation no longer applies: validate again.
::

### Actions on a selection

Tick several contacts to:

- **Validate selection** — checks the numbers on WhatsApp;
- **Sync groups** — adds or removes the ticked contacts from several groups at
  once;
- **Remove from all groups** — without deleting the contacts;
- **Delete** — permanent deletion.

**Delete all contacts** empties your database at once. It is irreversible: it does
not remove campaigns already sent, but it cuts your mailing list dead.

## Import and export

The import happens in **Contacts → Import**: see
[Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts) for the file format. A sample
file is downloadable from that page.

## Validate numbers

A **Pending** contact has not been checked on WhatsApp yet. The validation button
queries WhatsApp number by number and flips the status to **Validated** (the
number exists) or **Invalid** (it does not).

Validate:

- **before a first campaign** on a freshly imported list;
- **after changing a number**;
- **regularly** on old lists: numbers get deleted on WhatsApp too.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-gauge"}
Validation consumes WhatsApp requests. On a very large list, proceed in batches of
a few hundred rather than one single run.
::

A contact can also be attached to a **specific instance**: it will only be usable
with that instance. Leave it empty ("None") unless you have a reason.

## Groups

**Contact groups** works like a filing cabinet: a name, then contacts inside. A
contact can be in several groups.

- Create a group, name it after a commercial target ("Abidjan customers",
  "Prospects March 2026").
- Add contacts from the contact list (**Sync groups** on a selection) or from the
  group page.
- A campaign can target one or several groups at once.

**Sync from WhatsApp** imports the groups that already exist on your phone, with
their members, so you don't retype anything.

## Good practice

- One group per target, not one "everyone" group. You will segment later, and it
  is easier from the start.
- Delete **Invalid** contacts: they weigh on your sending.
- Never re-import a purchased list: it is the fastest route to a ban.

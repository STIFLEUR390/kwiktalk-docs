**Messages WhatsApp** is the history of everything that went through your
instances: messages you sent (campaigns and single messages) and messages your
customers sent you.

Access: left menu → **WhatsApp messages**

## What you see

Each line shows the direction (**incoming** or **outgoing**), the contact, the
type of content, the status and the date.

Filters:

- **status** — sent, delivered, read, failed;
- **direction** — received or sent.

The **search** matches a number or the content of a message.

## Message types

Text, image, audio, document, video, position and contact card. The type comes
from WhatsApp: if a customer sends you a photo, you see an image entry.

## Media

Images, audio and documents are **stored for a limited time**: after that, the
message stays in the list but the media has to be opened in WhatsApp itself. Save
what matters (an invoice, a proof of payment) while it is still available.

## Reading the conversation

Opening a message shows its content, its status and the instance used. To reply,
use [Single messages](/en/daily/messages) with the contact's number.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-bell"}
Replies to a campaign are the best signal on its quality: a message that triggers
replies is not a message that gets reported. Check this page after every send.
::

## When a message does not arrive

A received message does not appear:

1. check that the instance is **Connected** in **WhatsApp**;
2. check the **direction** filter: it may be hiding the entry;
3. wait a few minutes: WhatsApp sometimes delivers late.

→ [Troubleshooting](/en/help/troubleshooting)

KwikTalk warns you when something threatens your sends. The point is not to
inform you, it is to let you act before the damage spreads.

Access: left menu → **Notifications**

## The alerts you receive

| Alert | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Instance banned** | WhatsApp suspended the number, with a reason and an end date | Do not reconnect in a loop, see [Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban) |
| **Ban lifted** | The suspension ended | Reconnect, slow down, resume carefully |
| **Instance disconnected** | The linked device was removed or the phone was reset | Scan a new QR code |
| **Campaign paused by the anti-ban** | KwikTalk stopped a send it considered risky | Read the reason before resuming |

Each notification comes with a detailed email. Read the reason: it almost always
names the cause (sending pace, failure rate, recipient reports).

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
**Do not reconnect a banned instance over and over.** Each attempt can extend the
suspension. Wait for the stated end date, then reconnect once.
::

## The notifications list

The page keeps the history: date, type, instance and status. An unread
notification is signalled by a badge in the menu.

## Preferences

**Notification settings** lets you choose what you receive and how: in-app and/or
by email. Keep at least email on for **ban** and **disconnection** alerts: those
are the two that cost you money.

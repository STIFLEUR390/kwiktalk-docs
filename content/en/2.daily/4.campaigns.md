The creation of a campaign is described in
[Send your first campaign](/en/start/first-campaign). This page covers what
happens afterwards: following the send, resuming it, handling incidents.

Access: left menu → **Message campaigns**

## The campaign list

Each row shows the name, the status, the progress and the sending instance.
Filters let you isolate a status (*Running*, *Paused*, *Completed*) or a period.

## Reading the statuses

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Created, never launched | Edit it, launch it |
| **Scheduled** | Waiting for its start time | Cancel the schedule |
| **Running** | Sending in progress | Pause, cancel |
| **Paused** | Suspended (by you or by the anti-ban) | **Resume** |
| **Completed** | Every message was processed | Read the report, duplicate |
| **Cancelled** | Definitely stopped | Duplicate |
| **Failed** | The send could not happen | Read the error, duplicate |

On a running campaign, **progress** is shown as a percentage of the recipients
processed. Below, the recipients list shows each message's status: *Pending*,
*Sending*, *Sent*, *Delivered*, *Read*, *Failed*.

## Pause and resume

**Pause** stops the sending within seconds, without losing the campaign: the
remaining recipients stay queued. **Resume** picks up where it stopped.

Two reasons to pause:

- you want to fix a mistake in the template;
- KwikTalk itself paused the campaign (see below).

## The automatic anti-ban pause

KwikTalk monitors each instance. When a signal looks bad — too many failures in a
row, a slower WhatsApp, a suspicion of ban — it puts the campaign on hold **by
itself** and shows the reason (hourly limit reached, temporary suspension).

This is not a breakdown: it is protection. Do not resume right away:

1. read the stated reason;
2. check the instance status in **WhatsApp**;
3. read [Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban);
4. resume only when the instance is connected and the cause is understood.

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-shield-alert"}
Resuming a campaign over and over on an instance that WhatsApp is throttling is
the shortest route to a definitive ban. When in doubt, wait 24 hours.
::

## Cancel

**Cancel** stops the campaign for good: the remaining recipients will not be sent.
Messages already delivered are not recalled — WhatsApp has no undo.

## Reports

Two exports from the campaign:

- **CSV** — one line per recipient with their status. To open in Excel, count the
  failures, compare two campaigns.
- **PDF** — the campaign summary, to share or archive.

## What to check after a send

1. **Failure rate.** Above a few percent, the list is bad or the number is
   throttled. Validate your numbers, slow the pace.
2. **Replies.** They are in [Incoming messages](/en/daily/inbox). A campaign that
   gets no replies has a message problem, not a volume problem.
3. **Instance status.** If it moved to *Disconnected*, nothing more will send
   until you reconnect it.

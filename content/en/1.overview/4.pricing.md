KwikTalk offers **three plans**. Prices are in **West African CFA francs (XOF)**,
billed monthly or yearly.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-badge-check"}
The **Starter plan is free** with no time limit. Paid plans come with a
**14-day free trial** (Growth).
::

## The three plans

### Starter — 0 XOF

Discover KwikTalk on WhatsApp Web: 1 instance, 10 contacts and scheduled
campaigns with built-in anti-ban protection.

### Growth — 19,000 XOF/month · 183,000 XOF/year

For growing teams: 5 instances, 5,000 contacts, open API and automatic
rule-based chatbot. **14-day free trial.**

### Business — 65,000 XOF/month · 629,000 XOF/year

Complete solution: unlimited instances and contacts, open webhooks
(n8n, Make, CRM) and dedicated 24/7 support.

## Quota comparison

| Quota | Starter | Growth | Business |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| WhatsApp instances | 1 | 5 | ∞ |
| Contacts | 10 | 5,000 | ∞ |
| Campaigns | 2 | 20 | ∞ |
| Message templates | 5 | 50 | ∞ |
| API applications | 0 | 3 | ∞ |
| Webhooks | 0 | 3 | ∞ |
| Chatbots | 0 | 1 | ∞ |
| Chatbot rules | 0 | 20 | ∞ |
| Workflow nodes | 0 | 15 | ∞ |

## What really changes

| | Starter | Growth | Business |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **API access** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Chatbot** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Custom fields** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Advanced reports** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Anti-ban protection** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Support priority** | Standard | Priority | Dedicated 24/7 |

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
Without API access, the Starter plan **cannot create API applications or
webhooks** (`max_api_applications = 0`, `max_webhooks = 0`). To integrate
KwikTalk with your tools you need at least **Growth**.
::

::u-alert{color="success" icon="i-lucide-shield-check"}
**Anti-ban protection is active on all three plans**, including Starter. It is
never a hidden upsell.
::

## Manage your subscription

From your account:

- **Change plan** — `/subscriptions/manage`
- **View invoices** — `/subscriptions/invoices`
- **Track usage** — `/subscriptions/usage`
- **Apply a promo code** — entered before payment confirmation

See [Wallet and subscription](/en/account/wallet-subscription).

## In short

- **Starter** to test and start with a single number.
- **Growth** as soon as you need the API, a chatbot or more contacts.
- **Business** for unlimited multi-number and a full integration.

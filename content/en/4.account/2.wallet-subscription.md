Two distinct notions:

- the **wallet** — a prepaid balance, topped up whenever you want;
- the **subscription** — your plan (Starter, Growth, Business), billed monthly or
  yearly.

Access: **`/wallet`**, **`/subscriptions/*`**

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-shield"}
These pages are protected against **account takeover**: during an administrator
impersonation session, top-up and payment are **blocked**.
::

## The wallet

**`/wallet`** shows your balance; **`/wallet/transactions`** details the credit
and debit history (`balance_before`, `balance_after`, `credit`, `description`,
`reference`, `status`).

### Top up

1. From `/wallet`, choose an **amount**.
2. Choose the **payment method**.
3. You are redirected to the gateway.

**Suggested amounts in XOF**: `5,000` · `10,000` · `25,000` · `50,000`

**Maximum per top-up**: `2,000,000` XOF.

| Currency | Suggested amounts | Max per top-up |
|---|---|---|
| XOF | 5,000 · 10,000 · 25,000 · 50,000 | 2,000,000 |
| XAF | 5,000 · 10,000 · 25,000 · 50,000 | 2,000,000 |
| EUR | 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 | 10,000 |
| USD | 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 | 10,000 |

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-credit-card"}
Top-ups are currently only possible via **Flutterwave**. Stripe and PayPal pass
validation but return an explicit "not yet available" error. Plan for
Flutterwave.
::

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-clock"}
Your balance is **only credited after** the payment is confirmed by
Flutterwave's webhook. If the balance does move immediately, wait for the
confirmation before topping up again.
::

## The subscription

**`/subscriptions/manage`** is the entry point.

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Choose a plan | `/subscriptions/checkout/{plan}` |
| Change plan | `POST /subscriptions/swap` |
| Cancel | `POST /subscriptions/cancel` |
| Resume | `POST /subscriptions/resume` |
| View usage | `/subscriptions/usage` |
| Invoices | `/subscriptions/invoices` |

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-download"}
Every invoice is **downloadable**: the UI offers both a **direct download** and
a **PDF stream** (`stream`).
::

### Promo codes

A promo code is validated **before** payment confirmation:

```bash
POST /subscriptions/validate-coupon
```

The code is checked and applied to the amount. An expired, already-used or
inapplicable code is rejected — always check the response before paying.

### Changing plan

Changing plan immediately updates your **quotas** (instances, contacts,
campaigns, API, chatbot, webhooks). If you downgrade, make sure your current
resources fit the new caps — contacts or instances above the quota are not
deleted, but you cannot create more.

→ See the quota details: [Pricing](/en/overview/pricing).

## Payment callbacks

Gateways return to you at `/subscriptions/callback/{gateway}`. Never block this
route: it is what finalises the subscription.

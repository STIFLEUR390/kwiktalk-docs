The **dashboard** is the landing page of your workspace, available as soon as
your e-mail is verified.

Access: **`/dashboard`** · Statistics: **`/analytics`**

## The dashboard

The dashboard aggregates your account activity:

- the state of your **WhatsApp instances** (connected, disconnected, banned);
- your **contacts** and their volume;
- **campaigns** in progress and their progress;
- **messages** sent, delivered and failed;
- **pending incoming messages**.

It is the right starting point: at a glance you see what needs attention
(disconnected instance, paused campaign, failed messages).

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-gauge"}
The dashboard is open to every `view dashboard` permission, present in the
`user` role as well as the administrative roles.
::

## Statistics (`/analytics`)

The **Analytics** view drills into trends over time: send volumes, delivery
rates, activity per instance, status breakdown.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-chart-column"}
**Advanced reports** (`advanced_reports`) are reserved for the **Growth** and
**Business** plans. The **Starter** plan gets the dashboard but not the detailed
reports. See [Pricing](/en/overview/pricing).
::

## Onboarding

If the first-run tour was not finished, the dashboard reminds you to resume it.
Direct link: **`/onboarding`**.

See [First steps](/en/overview/first-steps).

## Feedback

From your workspace you can send **feedback** (`POST /feedback`) and review your
previous submissions. This is the channel for product suggestions and issues.

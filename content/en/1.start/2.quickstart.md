This tour takes you from the sign-up form to your first message sent. Allow about
**ten minutes**, most of it on your phone to scan the QR code.

## The five steps

| # | Step | Detailed page |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create your account and confirm your email | [Create an account](/en/start/create-account) |
| 2 | Connect your WhatsApp number | [Connect your number](/en/start/connect-whatsapp) |
| 3 | Check the message KwikTalk prepared for you | [Message templates](/en/daily/templates) |
| 4 | Send a test message to your own number | below |
| 5 | Import your contacts, then launch a campaign | [Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts) · [First campaign](/en/start/first-campaign) |

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-wand-2"}
Steps 2 to 4 happen inside a **getting-started assistant** that KwikTalk opens
automatically until your account is configured. You can leave it and come back
later: it resumes where you stopped.
::

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Open the sign-up page and fill in your **name**, your **email address** and a
   **password** (twice).
2. Open the confirmation email KwikTalk sends you and click the link.
3. Sign in: you land on the **dashboard**.

Details, lost email and signing in through your organisation:
[Create an account](/en/start/create-account).

## Step 2 — Connect WhatsApp

1. In the assistant, enter the **device name** (for example "Akwa shop") and your
   **WhatsApp number**.
2. KwikTalk displays a **QR code** and a **pairing code**.
3. On your phone: **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**, then
   scan the QR code — or enter the 8-character pairing code.
4. The status turns to **Connected**. Click **Continue**.

If the QR code expires: **Regenerate codes**, or **Check connection**.
Details: [Connect your number](/en/start/connect-whatsapp).

## Step 3 — Choose your message

KwikTalk **automatically creates a few message templates** when your account is
created. Pick the one that matches your business, customise the **greeting** and
your **company name**, then check the preview.

You can write your own later: [Message templates](/en/daily/templates).

## Step 4 — Send the test message

1. Enter the **recipient number** (your own, in international format).
2. Click **Send test message**.
3. Check on your phone that the message arrives.

::u-alert{color="success" icon="i-lucide-check-check"}
This test proves one thing: KwikTalk really talks to WhatsApp with your number. If
the message does not arrive while the status says "Connected", go to
[Troubleshooting](/en/help/troubleshooting).
::

## Step 5 — Your contacts, then your first campaign

1. **Contacts → Import**: drop your Excel file (columns `name` and
   `phone_number`). See [Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts).
2. **Campaigns → Create**: choose the instance, the template, the contact groups,
   set the delay between messages, then start. See
   [Send your first campaign](/en/start/first-campaign).

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-shield-alert"}
Do not send 500 messages to strangers on your first day. WhatsApp suspends numbers
that send too much, too fast. Read
[Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban) **before** your first large campaign.
::

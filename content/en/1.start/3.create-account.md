## Create an account

1. Open the KwikTalk sign-up page and click **Create an account**.
2. Fill in:
   - your **full name**;
   - your **email address**;
   - a **password**, then its **confirmation**.
3. Click **Create my account**.

### Confirm your email address

KwikTalk immediately sends a confirmation email. **Until you click the link it
contains, the dashboard stays closed.**

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-mail"}
Nothing received? Check your spam folder, then use the **resend confirmation
email** button shown on the verification page.
::

## Sign in

On the sign-in page, two options:

- **Email address + password** — the normal case. The "Forgot password?" link
  lets you set a new password by email.
- **Sign in through your organisation** — if your company uses an identity
  provider (the **Sign in with Authentik** or **Sign in with Keycloak** button),
  you are redirected to its sign-in page and then back to KwikTalk. Create the
  account only once this way, without a KwikTalk password.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-key-round"}
If **two-factor authentication** is enabled on your account, a second step asks
for a 6-digit code after your password.
::

## What happens right after sign-up

KwikTalk opens a **getting-started assistant** (`/onboarding`) until your account
is configured: connect WhatsApp, choose a template, send a test message. Until you
finish it, every sign-in takes you back there. Follow the
[quick tour](/en/start/quickstart).

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and submit.
3. Open the link you receive (it expires), choose a **new password**, then confirm.

If you sign in through Authentik or Keycloak, the password is reset at your
identity provider, not in KwikTalk.

## Change language

The interface language switcher toggles between **French** and **English**. Your
choice is kept on your account.

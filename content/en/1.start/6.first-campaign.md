A **campaign** sends one message to a set of contacts, with a delay between each
send. It is KwikTalk's main feature.

Access: left menu → **Message campaigns** → **Create a campaign**

## Before launching

- a **connected instance** — see [Connect your number](/en/start/connect-whatsapp);
- a **message template** — KwikTalk created a few automatically, otherwise see
  [Message templates](/en/daily/templates);
- a **recipient list**: either contact groups already imported, or an Excel file —
  see [Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts).

## Create the campaign

### 1. Message and sending number

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Message template** | The content sent. Required. |
| **WhatsApp instance** | The number that sends. One campaign = one instance. |
| **Campaign name** | Internal name, to find it in the list. |
| **Description** | Optional. |

### 2. Recipients

Two possible sources:

- **Contact groups** — tick one or several groups, the contact total shows up
  immediately.
- **Excel file** — drop a `.xlsx` or `.xls` file, then pick which column holds the
  phone numbers. KwikTalk shows a preview of the first row and the number of rows
  found: check that the chosen column is the right one before continuing.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-file-spreadsheet"}
The Excel import of a campaign is independent from your contact list: it does not
create contacts in KwikTalk, it only serves that campaign.
::

### 3. Pace and time

- **Delay between messages** — the wait between two sends (3 to 10 seconds, 5 by
  default). The longer the delay, the longer the campaign, and **the lower the
  risk of suspension**. Do not go below 5 seconds on a large list.
- **Schedule for later** — tick to choose a **date and time**, with the **time
  zone** in which that time is expressed. Without it, the campaign starts as soon
  as you click **Start**.

### 4. Check and launch

A **summary** shows the name, template, instance, contact source, contact count,
delay, time zone and planned time. Read it again, then click **Start campaign**
(or **Schedule campaign**).

## Track the send

The campaign page shows its **status** and its **progress as a percentage**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Created, not launched yet |
| **Scheduled** | Start planned at the chosen time |
| **Running** | Sending in progress |
| **Paused** | Suspended (you can resume it) |
| **Completed** | Every message has been processed |
| **Cancelled** | Definitely stopped |
| **Failed** | The send could not happen |

Each recipient has its own status: *Pending*, *Sending*, *Sent*, *Delivered*,
*Read* or *Failed*.

Two exports are available from the campaign:

- **CSV report** — the recipient list and their status, to open in Excel;
- **PDF report** — the full summary, to share.

## How long it takes

One message every 5 seconds is roughly **12 messages per minute**: 1 000 contacts
≈ 1 h 25, 5 000 contacts ≈ 7 h. A long campaign can therefore run across your
working day — that is normal, it keeps going on its own.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Launching 5 000 messages on a number you just connected.** On the first day,
  send a few dozen messages, then increase gradually.
- **Sending to a purchased list.** It is the first cause of bans.
- **Writing the exact same message thousands of times.** Use template variables to
  personalise.
- **Forgetting to validate numbers.** Every failure weighs on the number's
  reputation.

→ Read before your first large send:
[Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban).

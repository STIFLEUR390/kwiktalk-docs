An **instance** is a WhatsApp number connected to KwikTalk. It is what sends the
messages; contacts, templates and campaigns rely on it.

Access: left menu → **WhatsApp**

## Before you start

- An **active WhatsApp number**, with the phone in your hand.
- A 5-minute window: the QR code expires quickly.
- A plan allowing enough instances (1 on Starter, 5 on Growth, unlimited on
  Business — see [Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans)).

## Create the instance

1. Click **Create an instance**.
2. Enter the **instance name** — pick a meaningful one, it appears everywhere in
   the interface ("Akwa shop", "Support", "Sales 1").
3. Enter the **phone number** in international format.
4. Submit.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-link"}
The **Webhook URL** field is reserved for technical setups. If you don't know what
to put there, leave it empty: KwikTalk works fully without it.
::

## Link the phone

On the instance page, both methods are offered at the same time. Pick whichever
suits you.

### Method A — QR code

1. Click **Generate connection codes** (the instance must start as **Not
   connected**).
2. On the phone: **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**.
3. Scan the QR code displayed by KwikTalk.

### Method B — Pairing code

Useful when you cannot scan (phone held remotely, shared screen):

1. On the phone: **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link with phone
   number**.
2. Enter the **pairing code** displayed by KwikTalk.

In both cases the instance status turns to **Connected** within seconds. KwikTalk
checks the connection automatically.

## If the connection does not establish

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| The QR code expired | Click **Regenerate codes** |
| The scan worked but the status stays "Not connected" | Click **Check connection** |
| Pairing failed, nothing works | Click **Start over**: the linked device is removed on WhatsApp's side and you can scan again |
| The number was already linked to another tool | First remove the old linked device in WhatsApp, then try again |

## Keep the connection

- The link is a **multi-device** link: your phone can be off, WhatsApp Web keeps
  working.
- Never remove the "KwikTalk" device from WhatsApp linked devices: that stops
  your campaigns from sending.
- If WhatsApp disconnects the account (phone change, reinstall), the status turns
  to **Disconnected** and you get a notification. You have to scan a new QR code.

## Managing several numbers

Each instance is independent: contacts, campaigns and messages are attached to it.
A campaign picks **one** sending instance.

KwikTalk sends WhatsApp messages **from your own number**. You link your WhatsApp
account to the platform once, then you send messages to one contact or to a whole
list, from a dashboard.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-info"}
KwikTalk uses **WhatsApp Web (multi-device)**. Your phone does not need to stay on
or connected for messages to go out.
::

## The three building blocks

Everything else revolves around these three objects:

```
WhatsApp instance        ← the number that sends
├── Contacts             ← who receives (organised in groups)
└── Campaign             ← the message sent to several contacts
```

- An **instance** is a WhatsApp number connected to KwikTalk. You can connect
  several (one per service, per shop, per salesperson).
- A **contact** is a name and a number, filed in one or more **groups**.
- A **campaign** is a message sent to a group of contacts, right away or at a
  scheduled time.

## What you need

- An **active WhatsApp number** (a real number, not a disposable virtual number:
  WhatsApp blocks numbers it considers suspicious).
- A **valid email address**: it is used to confirm your account.
- A plan. The **Starter plan is free** — see [Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans).

A **ban** is WhatsApp suspending your number. It is the main risk of bulk
messaging, and it is almost always avoidable.

## Why WhatsApp bans a number

WhatsApp judges one thing: **do recipients want your messages?** Signals it
watches:

| Signal | What triggers it |
|---|---|
| **Recipient reports** | "Block and report" pressed by recipients |
| **No reply at all** | Hundreds of messages sent, zero answer |
| **Sudden volume** | 3 000 messages on a number that sent 20 the day before |
| **Identical content** | The same text, thousands of times, no personalisation |
| **Unvalidated numbers** | Mass sending to numbers that do not exist |
| **Suspicious links** | Shortened links, unknown domains |
| **Aggressive tone** | CAPS, "PROMO !!!", urgency |

The first three make up most bans. All three are under your control.

## Warm up a new number

A freshly connected number is not trusted yet. Give it a ramp:

| Period | Volume per day |
|---|---|
| Day 1–2 | A few dozen messages |
| Day 3–7 | 100 to 200 |
| Week 2 | 300 to 500 |
| Week 3 and beyond | Increase gradually |

During warm-up, **get replies**: ask a question, invite an answer. A number that
converses is a number WhatsApp trusts.

## The right settings

- **Delay between messages: 5 seconds minimum**, more on a large list. A campaign
  that "goes fast" is a campaign that gets reported.
- **Validated contacts only** — see
  [Contacts and groups](/en/daily/contacts).
- **Personalised messages** — `{name}` in every template.
- **Segmented groups** — send to 200 interested contacts, not to 5 000 unknowns.
- **Time slots** — send during business hours, not at 3 a.m.

## Anti-ban protection

KwikTalk ships a protection layer, **enabled by default**, that limits risky
behaviour automatically:

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Full protection** (default) | Daily and hourly quotas, per-recipient frequency, send delay with jitter, automatic pausing on a real ban |
| **Partial protection** | Preventive quotas lifted, ban handling kept |
| **Protection off** | No protection: you take the risk entirely |

Keep **Full protection**. It costs you a little pace and saves your number.

## A number was banned

1. **Do not reconnect in a loop.** Every attempt can extend the suspension.
2. Read the **reason** and the **end date** of the suspension in the
   notification.
3. Check your last campaign: volume? failures? content?
4. Wait for the end date, then reconnect **once**.
5. If the ban is permanent, the number is lost: use another number, and change
   what got it banned.

→ [Notifications](/en/daily/notifications)

## A healthy setup, in short

- validated contacts, segmented groups;
- personalised templates, one call to action;
- 5 seconds minimum between messages;
- a gradual ramp on a new number;
- anti-ban protection on;
- replies read and answered every day.

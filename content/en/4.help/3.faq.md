## Account

**Do I need an account to see the pricing?**
No. Pricing and the free tools are accessible without an account.

**Can I change my account's email address?**
Yes, in **Settings → Profile**. The new address has to be confirmed.

**I lost access to my phone with 2FA.**
Use a recovery code. Without a recovery code, contact support: identity
verification is mandatory.

## WhatsApp

**Does my phone have to stay on?**
No. KwikTalk uses WhatsApp in **multi-device** mode: once the device is linked,
sending works even with the phone off.

**Can I connect several numbers?**
Yes: 1 on Starter, 5 on Growth, unlimited on Business. Each instance is
independent.

**Can I use my personal WhatsApp number?**
Technically yes, but avoid it: a suspended campaign would also cut your personal
conversations. Use a number dedicated to the business.

**Does a virtual number work?**
Often poorly: WhatsApp blocks many virtual operators. Prefer a regular SIM card.

**Can I cancel a running campaign?**
Yes, by pausing it. Messages already sent cannot be recalled.

**Can I send an image or a PDF?**
Yes: templates accept an image (PNG, JPG, GIF) or a PDF, up to 10 MB.

**Do messages go out at weekends?**
Yes. A scheduled campaign starts at the chosen time, whatever the day.

## Contacts

**Does my Excel file need a specific format?**
Yes: `name` and `phone_number` columns required, `groups` optional, and a `.xlsx`
or `.xls` format.

**How many contacts can I have?**
10 on Starter, 5,000 on Growth, unlimited on Business.

**What does "Invalid" mean for a contact?**
The number does not exist on WhatsApp. The message will fail: fix or delete the
contact.

## Pricing

**Is the free plan time-limited?**
No. It is volume-limited: 1 instance, 10 contacts, 2 campaigns.

**Can I change plan at any time?**
Yes. The change is immediate and the new plan's quotas apply right away.

**What happens if I cancel?**
You keep access until the end of the paid period, then the account falls back to
Starter. Your data is not deleted.

**Can I pay by mobile money?**
Yes, depending on your country: card and mobile money payment is offered at
checkout, alongside bank cards.

## Messages and campaigns

**Why does my campaign send slowly?**
The delay between messages (5 seconds by default) is what protects your number.
1,000 contacts take a bit over an hour — that is intended.

**Can I edit a template used by a running campaign?**
No. Duplicate it, edit the copy, and use it for the next campaign.

**What is the maximum length of a message?**
4,096 characters. Beyond that, split it or use a PDF.

**Can I schedule a campaign for a specific date?**
Yes, with a date, a time and a time zone.

## API

**Is the API included?**
From the Growth plan (3 applications), unlimited on Business. Not on Starter.

**Does one key work with all my instances?**
No: an API key is attached to **one** instance.

**Where do I find my API key?**
**API applications**: it is displayed **once** when created. Then only its
prefix is visible.

Start from the symptom. Each entry says what to check, in order.

## I cannot connect my WhatsApp number

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| The QR code is not displayed | The instance is not in "Not connected" state | Click **Regenerate codes** |
| The code expires before scanning | Too slow | Regenerate, scan within a minute |
| The scan succeeds but nothing happens | The link is slow | Click **Check connection** |
| The pairing code is refused | The number already has too many linked devices | Remove an old device in WhatsApp, then retry |
| The number is linked elsewhere | Another tool uses it | Remove the old device in WhatsApp first |

→ [Connect your WhatsApp number](/en/start/connect-whatsapp)

## My campaign does not start

1. **Status of the instance** — it must be *Connected*. A disconnected instance
   sends nothing.
2. **Status of the template** — it must be *Active*. A draft does not appear in the
   picker.
3. **Recipients** — a group with no contact, or a wrong column picked in the Excel
   file, gives zero recipients.
4. **Scheduled campaign** — check the date, the time **and the time zone**.
5. **Quota** — the plan's campaign limit may be reached: see
   [Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans).

## My messages fail

The recipient's status shows *Failed*:

| Probable cause | Check |
|---|---|
| Invalid number | Contact status is *Invalid*: the number does not exist on WhatsApp |
| Wrong format | International format **without `+`**, e.g. `2250701020304` |
| Instance disconnected mid-send | Instance status in **WhatsApp** |
| Number blocked by the recipient | Nothing to do: move on |
| Temporary WhatsApp throttling | Wait, slow the pace, see [Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban) |

A high failure rate (more than a few percent) always means a bad list or a
throttled number. Do not relaunch as is.

## The campaign paused by itself

That is the **anti-ban protection**: KwikTalk judged the send risky and stopped
it. Read the stated reason, check the instance, wait, then resume. Resuming
immediately is the best way to get banned.

→ [Campaigns](/en/daily/campaigns) · [Avoiding bans](/en/help/anti-ban)

## The menu entry I need is missing

The menu adapts to your **plan** and your **rights**:

- **Chatbot** and **API applications** do not exist on Starter;
- an entry can be hidden if your subscription is **overdue**.

→ [Plans and quotas](/en/billing/plans) · [Subscription](/en/billing/subscription)

## I forgot my password

**Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, then follow the emailed link. If you sign
in through Authentik or Keycloak, the password is reset at your organisation.

→ [Create an account and sign in](/en/start/create-account)

## My imports are rejected

The file must be **`.xlsx` or `.xls`**, with the headers **`name`** and
**`phone_number`** written exactly like that on the first row. `groups` is
optional. No CSV.

→ [Import your contacts](/en/start/import-contacts)

## The statistics page looks empty

Analytics need a few days of activity. A message from the page telling you
analytics are unavailable means the reporting service is being restarted: the data
comes back by itself, nothing to do.

## Still stuck

Contact support from the website, giving: the instance concerned, the campaign
name, the approximate time, and the exact error message displayed. Three precise
lines save a day of back and forth.

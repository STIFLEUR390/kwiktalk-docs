The **wallet** is your prepaid balance in KwikTalk. It is what pays your
subscription; it is not the subscription itself.

Access: left menu → **Subscription & billing → Wallet**

## What the wallet is for

- pay the **subscription** of the current month;
- pay an **upgrade** to a higher plan;
- settle any **additional** service offered in the interface.

Every payment is recorded in the transaction history: date, amount, method,
status. A **successful** transaction debits the wallet; a **failed** one changes
nothing.

## Top up

1. **Wallet → Top up**.
2. Enter the **amount**.
3. Choose the **payment method**:
   - **Card (Stripe)** — international cards;
   - **Card and mobile money (Flutterwave)** — Orange Money, MTN, Moov, Wave
     depending on the country;
   - **Internal wallet** — transfer from an existing balance;
   - **PayPal**.
4. Confirm: you are redirected to the provider's payment page, then back to
   KwikTalk.

The balance updates as soon as the provider confirms. If the money left your
account but the balance did not move, wait a few minutes, then check the
transaction history before contacting support.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-receipt"}
Use the **same** email address at the payment provider and in KwikTalk: it makes
reconciliation of a failed top-up much easier.
::

## Transaction history

The list shows every movement with its **status**:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | The amount was credited or debited |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the provider's confirmation |
| **Failed** | Nothing was debited |
| **Refunded** | The amount was returned |

Each line can be opened for the detail (reference, method, date). Statements are
available as a **PDF**.

## Common questions

**My balance is zero but I paid.** Check the transaction status. If it is
*Pending*, the provider has not confirmed yet; if it is *Failed*, no money was
taken and you can start again.

**Which amount should I top up?** At least the price of your plan for the next
period. Paying several months at once is allowed: the balance is drawn down
monthly.

**Is the balance refundable?** The wallet is a prepaid service credit: check the
conditions on the website before topping up a large amount.

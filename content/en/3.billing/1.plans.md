KwikTalk works with **plans**. A plan sets how many objects you can create
(instances, contacts, campaigns, templates, API applications, chatbots) and which
features you can use.

## The three plans

| | **Starter** | **Growth** | **Business** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | Free | 19,000 FCFA | 65,000 FCFA |
| Yearly price | Free | 183,000 FCFA | 629,000 FCFA |
| Free trial | — | 14 days | 14 days |
| WhatsApp instances | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Contacts | 10 | 5,000 | Unlimited |
| Campaigns | 2 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Message templates | 5 | 50 | Unlimited |
| API applications | — | 3 | Unlimited |
| Chatbot | — | 1 chatbot, 20 rules | Unlimited |
| Support | Standard | Priority | Dedicated 24/7 |
| Anti-ban protection | Yes | Yes | Yes |

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-info"}
Prices are in **FCFA (XOF)**. Paying yearly costs less than twelve monthly
payments. The applicable prices are always the ones on the **Pricing** page of the
website.
::

## Check your usage

Access: **Subscription & billing → Usage** (or from the dashboard, **See all
limits**).

The table shows, for each feature, your **limit**, what you have **used** and what
is **left**.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | You are far from the limit |
| **Nearly reached** | Little room left |
| **Limit reached** | You cannot create a new object of this kind |

## When a limit is reached

KwikTalk **deletes nothing**: you simply cannot create a new object. Existing
contacts, campaigns already created and messages already sent keep working.

Three options:

1. **Delete useless objects** — completed campaigns, unused templates: the counter
   frees up immediately.
2. **Change plan** — see [Subscription and invoices](/en/billing/subscription).
3. **Wait for the renewal** of your period, for counters that reset.

## What the plan does not limit

- The **number of messages sent** is not capped by the plan. What you pay for is
  your account's capacity (instances, contacts), not the volume.
- The **number of contact groups** is not limited.

## The trial

Growth and Business come with a **14-day free trial**: full featured, without a
payment method required to start. At the end of the trial, choose a plan to keep
using them — otherwise the account falls back to Starter's limits.

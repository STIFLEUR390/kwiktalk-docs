Everything about your personal account is under **Settings**.

Access: left menu → **Settings**

## Profile

**Settings → Profile**

- **Full name** — shown in the interface.
- **Email address** — your sign-in address. Changing it requires a **new
  confirmation**: click the link in the email sent to the new address.
- **WhatsApp number** — your own contact number, used to reach you. It is not an
  instance: it does not send campaigns.
- **Resend verification email** — if the confirmation never arrived.

## Password

**Settings → Security**

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Choose a **new password** (long enough, a passphrase is better than a complex
   short one).
3. Confirm and save.

If you sign in through **Authentik** or **Keycloak**, the password is managed at
your organisation and cannot be changed here.

## Two-factor authentication

**Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**

A second factor protects your account even if your password leaks.

1. Click **Enable**.
2. Scan the **QR code** with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy,
   1Password, etc.), or type the secret key by hand.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** displayed by the app to confirm.
4. **Save the recovery codes** shown: they are your only way back in if you lose
   the phone.

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-key-round"}
The recovery codes are displayed **once**. Store them away from the phone
(password manager, printed in a safe). Without them, losing the phone means
contacting support.
::

**Disable** two-factor at any time from the same screen, with your password.

## Appearance

**Settings → Appearance** — **Light**, **Dark** or **System** theme. The choice is
stored on your account.

## Delete your account

**Settings → Delete account** permanently removes your account and its data:
instances, contacts, campaigns, templates, messages. Invoices are kept as required
by law.

This action is **irreversible**. Export what you need first (CSV campaign reports,
contact lists) and make sure no subscription is still running.

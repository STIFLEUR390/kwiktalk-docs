The **subscription** is your current plan. It renews on its own as long as your
wallet can pay for it.

Access: left menu → **Subscription & billing → Subscription**

## The four states

| State | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | The plan is paid and working | Nothing |
| **Pending payment** | A payment is expected to activate the plan | Top up and pay |
| **Payment overdue** | The renewal could not be paid | Top up quickly, access may be suspended |
| **Expired** | The subscription ended | Reactivate to get your limits back |

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
An unpaid subscription does not delete your data. It lowers your account to the
Starter limits: above 10 contacts, you can no longer create new ones, but nothing
is lost.
::

## Change plan

1. **Subscription → Change plan**.
2. Pick the target plan.
3. Confirm and pay the difference, or the first period.

The change is immediate. **Going down** a plan takes effect at the end of the
current period: you keep the higher plan until then.

## Cancel

**Cancel subscription** stops the renewal. Your access continues **until the end of
the period already paid**: cancelling does not reimburse the current period and
does not cut access the same day.

After that date, the account falls back to Starter limits, with data kept.

## Resume

A cancelled subscription can be **resumed** before its end date: it simply
continues. After the end date, use **Reactivate** and pay a new period.

## Invoices

Every period produces an invoice, downloadable as **PDF**, with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | Settled |
| **Draft** | Not issued yet |
| **Pending** | Waiting for payment |
| **Overdue** | Not paid on time |
| **Refunded** | Refunded |
| **Cancelled** | Voided |

Invoices are the accounting document: keep them for your bookkeeping.

## Promo codes

A **promo code** field is available when subscribing or topping up. A code applies
a discount or a free period, depending on the campaign. One code per order. If a
code is refused, check its expiry date and whether it is limited to a first
subscription.

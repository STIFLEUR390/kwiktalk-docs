Contacts are the base of every campaign. They are bound to a **WhatsApp
instance**, since that is what will send the messages.

Access: **`/admin/contacts`** and **`/admin/contact-groups`**

## Add a contact

Two ways:

- **Manual creation** — name + phone number.
- **Excel import** (see below).

## Import from an Excel file

1. Go to **Contacts → Import**.
2. Drop your file. Accepted formats: **`.xlsx` and `.xls`**, maximum size
   **10 MB**.
3. **Column headers** are detected automatically — you can map each column to the
   right field.
4. Preview each column before confirming.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-file-spreadsheet"}
An **example template** is downloadable from the import screen
(`contacts.import.example`): start from that file to avoid column-mapping issues.
::

Excel import is also available **inside a campaign**, to load recipients at
creation time.

## Validate numbers

A contact is marked `is_validated` with a `validation_date` once its number has
been **confirmed as active on WhatsApp**.

- **Single validation** — from a contact's detail page.
- **Bulk validation** — select several contacts, then **Validate**.

This is strongly recommended **before a campaign**: sending to non-existent
numbers degrades your number's reputation and raises the ban risk.

## Sync with WhatsApp

From the instance, **Sync contacts** imports the contacts present on your
WhatsApp account. From contacts, **Sync groups** pulls your chat groups.

## Detach vs delete

Two distinct actions — do not confuse them:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Detach** | Removes the contact from the group or instance, **without deleting it** |
| **Delete** | Destroys the contact record permanently |

Bulk variants exist: **detach all**, **delete all**, **delete and detach**, and a
**bulk desync**.

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-triangle-alert"}
Bulk deletes are **permanent**. Export or note your lists before running them.
::

## Contact groups

**Groups** segment your base ("Lyon customers", "Q3 prospects") and are usable as
a **campaign target**.

A group can contain:

- contacts added one by one (**Attach contacts**),
- contacts detached individually,
- contacts synced from WhatsApp.

## Custom fields

The **Growth** and **Business** plans support **custom fields**
(`custom_fields`): add your own columns (sector, city, amount due…) and use them
in [message templates](/en/features/message-templates).

The **Starter** plan does not.

The most frequent questions, as asked on the KwikTalk site (**`/faq`**).

## How does number validation work?

The platform checks whether a **number exists on WhatsApp** through the
Evolution API and returns a **validated** / **not validated** status.

This filters out inactive numbers before a campaign, which protects the
reputation of your sending number.

→ See [Contacts and groups](/en/features/contacts-groups) for single and bulk
validation, and [API applications](/en/integrations/api-applications) for the
`POST /api/v1/contacts/verify` endpoint.

## Is my data secure?

Yes — data is encrypted end to end and stored on **GDPR-compliant** servers.

Details are on the [Security](/en/resources/security) page: TLS/SSL encryption,
revocable API keys, access control, daily backups.

## Can I use several WhatsApp numbers?

Yes, KwikTalk lets you connect and manage **several instances at once**.

How many depends on your plan:

| Plan | Instances |
|---|:---:|
| Starter | 1 |
| Growth | 5 |
| Business | ∞ |

→ See [Pricing](/en/overview/pricing) and
[WhatsApp instances](/en/features/whatsapp-instances).

## Is there a free trial?

Yes, a **14-day free trial** is available on every paid plan, and the
**Starter plan stays free** with no time limit.

→ See [Pricing](/en/overview/pricing).

## A question that is not here?

per minute per IP (anti-spam protection).

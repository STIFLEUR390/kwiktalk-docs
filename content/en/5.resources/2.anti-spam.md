WhatsApp suspends numbers that send too many messages too fast. KwikTalk
includes **anti-ban protection** active on **all plans**, including Starter.

Related public pages: **`/anti-spam`** and **`/prevenir-bannissement`**

## The three protection modes

The protection level is set by a **mode** (`AntiBanMode`):

| Mode | Preventive quotas | Ban handling |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **`full`** *(default)* | ✅ | ✅ |
| **`preventive_off`** | — | ✅ |
| **`off`** | — | — |

### `full` — the default

Two components:

1. **Preventive quotas** — sends are **limited before** anything goes wrong:
   - **daily** budget,
   - **hourly** budget,
   - **per-recipient frequency** (do not hammer a single number),
   - **batch size**,
   - **jitter** (slight randomness in the delays, so the rhythm does not look
     robotic).
2. **Real ban handling** — if the number does get banned anyway:
   - **automatic pause** of the affected campaigns,
   - immediate **notification**,
   - **sending blocked** for the duration of the ban.

### `preventive_off`

Preventive quotas are **disabled**, but **reactive handling** remains: if a ban
happens, campaigns pause and sends are blocked. The number stays protected
**after** the ban.

### `off`

**No protection at all.** Sends are neither limited nor blocked, even during an
active ban.

::u-alert{color="danger" icon="i-lucide-skull"}
**Risk: permanent ban of the number.** This mode only makes sense for a
controlled use, on a number whose loss you accept. It is discouraged in
production.
::

## Warning signs and reactions

| Sign | What happens |
|---|---|
| Instance disconnected | Check the status and reconnect (`refresh-status`) |
| `instance_banned` | Notification sent, campaigns auto-paused |
| `ban_lifted` | Ban-ended notification, sending can resume |
| `session_unstable` | The WhatsApp connection is unstable |

An instance exposes `banned_at`, `banned_until` and `ban_reason`: you know
**why** and **until when**.

See [WhatsApp instances](/en/features/whatsapp-instances).

## Healthy sending practices

1. **Validate numbers before a campaign** — sending to non-existent numbers
   degrades reputation. See [Contacts and groups](/en/features/contacts-groups).
2. **Lower the batch size and raise the delay** — the most effective setting.
   See [Messages and campaigns](/en/features/messages-campaigns).
3. **Segment**: several small campaigns beat one big one.
4. **Personalise** — generic bulk messages trigger user reports. Use `{name}` in
   your [templates](/en/features/message-templates).
5. **Watch the replies** — a high blocking rate shows up in
   [incoming messages](/en/features/incoming-messages).
6. **Do not disable protection** without a reason: `full` exists for you.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-heart-handshake"}
A healthy WhatsApp number **receives as much as it sends**. The best protection
is a real conversation, not just a quota.
::

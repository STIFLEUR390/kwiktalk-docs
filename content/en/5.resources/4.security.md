Public page **`/securite`**. Here are the platform's guarantees and the
practices we recommend on your side.

## The six pillars

### End-to-end encryption

All communication between your browser and the servers is encrypted with
**TLS/SSL**. Messages going through WhatsApp benefit from the platform's native
encryption.

### API key management

Each application gets **unique, revocable API keys**. Keys are stored securely —
server-side they are only kept **SHA-256 hashed** — and can be regenerated at any
time from your dashboard.

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-key-round"}
A key cannot be "recovered": if you lose it, you must **regenerate** one, which
invalidates the previous key. See
[API applications](/en/integrations/api-applications).
::

### Data privacy

Your contacts, messages and data are **never shared with third parties**. You keep
full ownership of your data. **GDPR** compliance guaranteed.

### Secure infrastructure

Hosted on secure servers, with:

- **daily backups**,
- **24/7 monitoring**,
- **disaster recovery plans**.

Service status is public: [Platform status](/en/resources/status).

### Access control

Fine-grained permission management for your team members. Every user has
**specific roles and access** to platform features.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-users"}
Rights are granted by **permission**, not by a fixed menu: a `user` role holds
every operational permission (instances, contacts, messages, campaigns, API,
chatbot, webhooks) but not account administration.
::

### Send stability

A **queue** system and **automatic retries** ensure deliverability. **Rate
limiting** prevents your WhatsApp instances from being blocked.

The same logic powers [anti-spam protection](/en/resources/anti-spam) and the
automatic resume of [campaigns](/en/features/messages-campaigns).

## Recommended best practices

1. Use **different API keys per application**.
2. Enable **two-factor authentication** on your account.
3. **Regularly review** access to your dashboard.
4. **Limit permissions** to the strict minimum.
5. Keep your **QR connection codes** in a safe place.
6. Use **strong, unique passwords**.

::u-alert{color="warning" icon="i-lucide-smartphone"}
The **linking QR code** is equivalent to an open session on your WhatsApp
account. Do not photograph it in an accessible place, and use **Reset the
instance** if you suspect it was compromised. See
[WhatsApp instances](/en/features/whatsapp-instances).
::

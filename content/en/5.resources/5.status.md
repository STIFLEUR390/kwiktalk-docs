Public page **`/statut`**: follow service availability in real time.

## What the page shows

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **All systems operational** | No known issue |
| **Maintenance upcoming** | An operation is scheduled |
| **No maintenance scheduled** | Nothing planned right now |

The page also shows the **last updated date**.

## When to check it

- **Before deploying a campaign** — make sure no maintenance is announced before
  starting a large send.
- **If your sends feel unusually slow** — a running incident may explain the
  latency.
- **After an incident** — to confirm things are back to normal.

## In case of an incident

1. Check the platform **status**.
2. Inspect your **WhatsApp instances** — a disconnection may be local and
   unrelated to the incident: see
   [WhatsApp instances](/en/features/whatsapp-instances).
3. Review the **failed messages** and the instance **DLQ** to replay whatever
   failed during the outage.

::u-alert{color="info" icon="i-lucide-bell"}
Critical events (banned instance, payment, campaign completion) also raise
[notifications](/en/account/notifications) on the channels you chose.
::
